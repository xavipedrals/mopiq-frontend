GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about Mopiq

From your first import to your next review.

01What can I do with Mopiq?+

Create flashcards from your own material, organize them into decks, then study with spaced repetition. You can also practice with quizzes and track your study activity.

02How is Mopiq different from a general AI chatbot?+

Mopiq saves the cards you make into editable decks and keeps track of when each card is due. You can return to the same deck for reviews and quizzes without rebuilding it each time.

03How do active recall and spaced repetition work together?+

Try to answer the front of a card from memory. Flip it, then choose Again, Hard, Good, or Easy. Mopiq uses your rating to bring harder cards back sooner and space out the cards you know well.

04Can I make cards from my own study materials?+

Yes. Start from a PDF, slides, a Word document, notes, photos, or audio. You can also start from a topic, paste text, or write cards yourself. Import options vary by platform.

05Can I bring over decks from Anki?+

Yes. Export a deck from Anki as an .apkg file and use Mopiq’s Anki import. On the web, this creates a deck from the cards. Your Anki review history stays in Anki.

06What subjects and languages can I study?+

Use Mopiq for anything you can turn into questions and answers. Try vocabulary in the language you’re learning, a diagram for anatomy, or short prompts for science or history.

07Can I start free, and what comes with Pro?+

Yes. Start with a free account to create decks and review flashcards. Check the upgrade screen in Mopiq for the current limits, features, and prices for your platform.

08Can I use Mopiq on more than one device?+

You can use Mopiq on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and in a browser. Sign in with the same account to access your decks across devices. A native Android app is in development, and you can use the web app on Android today.

09Can I see how my studying is going?+

Mopiq shows your review activity and study time so you can see how often you’re studying and what’s left to review.

10Where can I read about how Mopiq handles my data?+

The Mopiq Privacy Policy explains how the service handles your information.
